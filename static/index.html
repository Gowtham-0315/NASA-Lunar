Canada's Vision for Lunar Exploration

Canada is an active partner in international lunar exploration. Through the Canadian Space Agency, Canadian industry (Canadensys Aerospace) and universities are developing a micro-rover designed to land at the lunar South Pole, search for water ice in permanently shadowed regions (PSRs), and survive extreme lunar conditions.

🤖 Canadarm3 on NASA's Lunar Gateway

Canadarm3 Autonomous Space Robotics System

Canadarm3 is Canada's next-generation contribution to NASA's Lunar Gateway space station. Operating from Near Rectilinear Halo Orbit (NRHO), Canadarm3 provides autonomous robotic inspection, maintenance, scientific payload transfers, and satellite communication relay to lunar surface rovers during periods when crater rims occlude direct line-of-sight to Earth.

Orbit: NRHO (70,000 km South Pole apolune) Operations: CSA Mission Control (Saint-Hubert, Quebec) Surface Relay: High-elevation polar line-of-sight coverage
